Six services, one closed loop

Most clients start with an audit and grow into reuse. Every service below can be bought on its own — but the savings compound when they run together on the Sentinel platform.

SERVICE 01

Water audits & baselining

Before anyone quotes a reuse plant, we know where every litre goes. A two-week survey replaces guesswork with sub-metered, end-use data and a board-ready savings model.

2-week survey End-use breakdown Board-ready model

Twelve months of utility history review, temporary ultrasonic sub-metering of 8–14 end uses, cooling and boiler log analysis, and a prioritised savings register with capex, payback and confidence scoring for each measure.

The audit fee itself is recovered in under nine months on average, because 60–70% of identified savings are low- or no-capex operational changes such as cycle tuning and leak repair.

Twelve months of billing and usage records, roof and riser access during low-occupancy hours, and one facilities contact who knows the mechanical rooms.

SERVICE 02

Greywater & blackwater reuse

Treatment trains sized to your actual load — membrane bioreactors, UV and ozone — delivering reuse water for toilet flushing, cooling make-up, laundry and irrigation.

MBR & UV/ozone Dual-plumbing PE-stamped design

Process design, mechanical and electrical drawings, permitting support, equipment supply, phased installation with our own crews, operator certification training and a twelve-month performance warranty.

2.5–4.5 years depending on local tariff, sewer structure and irrigation demand. Hospitals and hotels with 24-hour hot-water loads sit at the fast end.

Plant-room or yard footprint (typically 400–900 sq ft), a electrical service spare capacity, and a window for phased work — most installs run across weekends.

SERVICE 03

Process water recycling

Closed-loop recovery for brewing, food processing, plating and rinsing lines — where 80–94% of process water can go back into service without touching product quality.

CIP recovery Side-stream filtration QA-safe

Line-by-line mass balance, treatability trials at our Reno lab, integration with your existing CIP and rinse stages, and operator procedures written to your QA programme rather than ours.

1.8–3 years. In high-tariff regions with sewer surcharges, we have recorded paybacks inside 14 months (Ferrofield Brewing: 14 months, $418k annual saving).

Batch schedules, water-quality specifications per stage, QA sign-off contact, and 20 litres of representative process water for lab trials.

SERVICE 04

Cooling tower & boiler optimisation

The quietest savings on most sites live in cycles of concentration. We tune conductivity, filtration and blowdown control to cut make-up water and chemical spend together.

Conductivity control Side-stream filtration Chemical review

Existing-programme review, conductivity set-point engineering, blowdown controller configuration, filtration retrofit, and a monthly chemistry report your operator can act on in five minutes.

6–18 months. Multi-tower campuses with old conductivity controllers recover fastest — make-up reductions of 18–35% are typical in the first quarter.

Twelve months of chemistry logs, controller access, chemical vendor contracts, and a facilities escort for the tower inspection.

SERVICE 05

Smart metering & leak detection

Ultrasonic sub-meters streaming to Sentinel every 15 minutes, with anomaly alerts that surface hidden leaks days before the monthly bill would.

±1.5% accuracy 15-min intervals 72-hr alert lead

Meter schedule design, clamp-on ultrasonic installation with no pipe cuts, gateway and cellular connectivity, Sentinel onboarding, alert tuning and a monthly exceptions report.

9–20 months. The first leak a client catches inside the first quarter usually covers the whole metering programme for that year.

Riser and plant-room access, cellular signal check at gateway locations, and an email distribution list for alert recipients.

SERVICE 06

Compliance & certification support

Reuse water is regulated. We keep your permits, monitoring and evidence files audit-ready — and translate them into LEED, WELL and ISO 46001 credit.

Permit packs Pathogen monitoring ISO 46001

State permit application preparation, sampling plan and lab coordination, record-keeping templates, annual reporting, and credit documentation for LEED v4.1, WELL and ISO 46001 audits.

Not sold as a saving — it protects the saving. Clients typically recover 10–20 staff hours per month once Sentinel automates the evidence file.

Existing permits and monitoring records, your safety officer's contact details, and access to the building's water quality log book.

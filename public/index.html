How we work

A five-step method that de-risks every project

No surprise shutdowns, no speculative capex. Each stage produces evidence before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Sub-metered survey of every end use, plus 12 months of utility history.

  2. 02

    Model

    Hydraulic and payback modelling across three reuse scenarios.

  3. 03

    Engineer

    Mechanical, electrical and controls drawings stamped by licensed PEs.

  4. 04

    Commission

    Phased install during your low-load window, with operator training.

  5. 05

    Monitor

    Sentinel watches every litre and reports savings monthly.

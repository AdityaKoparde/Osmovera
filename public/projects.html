How these numbers are produced

We do not report modelled savings. Every case study on this page is measured by calibrated third-party meters, audited at the twelve-month mark, and signed off by the client's own facilities director before publication.

  • Sub-metered baselines covering a full twelve months before work begins
  • Independent metering for twelve months after commissioning
  • Savings adjusted for occupancy, weather and production volume
  • Client countersignature required before any figure is published

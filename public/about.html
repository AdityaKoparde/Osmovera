We are 34 people obsessed with the second life of water

Osmovera was founded in 2016 by a hospital facilities engineer and a membrane chemist who were tired of watching potable water go down the drain — literally.

Our story

It started with a leak nobody could find

In 2015, our co-founder Priya Raghunathan was running facilities at a 400-bed hospital whose water bill had climbed 31% in a year. Every consultant blamed the laundry. The real culprit was a fractured PRV loop nobody had sub-metered.

She partnered with membrane chemist Dr. Ingrid Sørensen to answer a bigger question: if a building can measure every litre, why is most of it still used once and discarded? Osmovera was formed the following spring to close that loop.

Ten years on, we have commissioned more than 640 reuse systems across 11 states, reclaimed 3.8 billion gallons, and never once had a client shut down operations for an installation.

What we believe

  • Measure first. No reuse system should be designed before twelve months of sub-metered data exist.
  • Water is a balance-sheet item. Every project must clear a real payback hurdle, not a vanity metric.
  • Operations beat brochures. A system nobody can operate is a liability, however green it looks.
  • Prove it in public. Third-party verification of savings is standard on every engagement.

Our standards

How we hold ourselves accountable

Safety without exceptions

Zero recorded waterborne-exposure incidents across 640 commissionings, and daily pathogen monitoring on every live reuse line.

Evidence over claims

Savings are measured by third-party meters for twelve months after handover. If we miss the modelled figure, we remediate at our cost.

Local, licensed, accountable

Every drawing is stamped by a professional engineer licensed in the project's state. Our field crews are employees, never subcontractors.
